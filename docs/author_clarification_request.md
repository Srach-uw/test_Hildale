# Minimal author data request

The final AJ article and machine-readable Table 1 resolve the host sample,
kinematics, disk probabilities, and ALDERAAN release. Three unpublished inputs
still prevent an exact planet-level replication.

1. **Accepted planet ledger.** Could you share the final list of KIC and KOI
   identifiers, periods, multiplicity labels, and transit-fit inclusion flags?
   Our reconstruction reaches the published total of 2,465 planets, but the
   category membership differs slightly and the final rejected systems are not
   identified publicly.
2. **Stellar-density inputs.** Could you share the density value and uncertainty
   used for each planet, together with the catalog columns or calculation that
   produced them? The methods cite Berger et al. (2018), while other article
   material points to Berger et al. (2020); the two routes do not provide an
   interchangeable density prior.
3. **Eccentricity and hierarchy products.** Could you share either the final
   per-planet `(e, omega)` posterior samples or the population-model code and
   ordered input arrays used for Table 3? Either product would identify whether
   the remaining difference enters before or during hierarchical inference.

The public ALDERAAN v0.1.0 archive is code-equivalent to the commit used here,
so an ALDERAAN version mismatch is no longer an open issue.
